# INC-1873: pg-feeds primary disk failure and repmgr switchover

Date: 2025-03-11, 13:41 to 14:36 UTC. Severity: sev2. IC: mbe.

## Summary

The data disk on pg-feeds-1 (primary) started returning I/O errors. Writes
slowed, feed-builder fell behind and `FeedBuildLagHigh` fired. We switched
the primary to pg-feeds-2 with repmgr following runbook postgres-failover
v2. Total write pause 6 minutes. No data lost.

## Impact

- Episode publishing delayed up to 22 minutes between 13:41 and 14:20.
- Feed reads were unaffected (served from cache and replicas).

## Timeline (UTC)

- 13:41 `FeedBuildLagHigh` fires. mbe on call.
- 13:44 `NodeFilesystemAlmostOutOfSpace` on pg-feeds-1 (the filesystem went
  read-only, which the exporter reports as no space).
- 13:52 dmesg on pg-feeds-1 shows I/O errors on the data volume. Decision: switch over.
- 13:58 feed-builder scaled to 0 (`kubectl -n feeds scale deploy/feed-builder --replicas=0`).
- 14:01 Dry run on pg-feeds-2:
  `repmgr -f /etc/repmgr.conf standby switchover --siblings-follow --dry-run`.
  Prerequisites met.
- 14:02 `repmgr -f /etc/repmgr.conf standby switchover --siblings-follow`
- 14:03 `repmgr -f /etc/repmgr.conf cluster show`: pg-feeds-2 primary,
  pg-feeds-1 and pg-feeds-3 standby, all running.
- 14:06 pgbouncer-feeds ConfigMap updated to the new primary, rolling restart.
- 14:07 feed-builder scaled back to 12. Writes resume.
- 14:36 Lag cleared, resolved.

## What went well

- The v2 runbook worked as written. `--siblings-follow` kept pg-feeds-3
  following the new primary.
- Dry run first caught nothing, which is what you want.

## What went badly

- Editing the PgBouncer ConfigMap by hand under pressure. We typed the wrong
  hostname the first time and lost a minute.
- pg-feeds-1's disk had logged warnings for two days. Nobody was alerted.
