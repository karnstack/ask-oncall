# INC-2276: pg-feeds leader on a degraded volume; #ask-oncall cited a deleted runbook

2026-10-21, 02:38 to 03:05 UTC. Sev3. IC and author: hq.

## What happened

The volume under pg-feeds-0, the Patroni leader, went slow (about 900 ms
I/O wait, kernel I/O errors). Patroni did not fail over because the leader
was alive and renewing its lock. feed-builder fell behind and
`FeedBuildLagHigh` fired at 02:38.

I asked `#ask-oncall` "how do I fail over postgres?". It answered with the
repmgr steps and cited postgres-failover.md v2. I ran the dry run on
pg-feeds-1 and got `repmgr: command not found`. It took me until 02:46 to
find runbook v3 (Patroni) on my own. Then the switchover took 6 minutes
following v3 and worked.

Lost time from the wrong answer: about 4 minutes.

## Why #ask-oncall said repmgr

From dara:

- v2 was deleted from the runbooks registry on 2026-06-18, when v3 was
  published. The `#ask-oncall` index was first built on 2026-05-04 and the
  nightly job re-reads every source and upserts the chunks it finds. A
  deleted document produces no chunks, so nothing ever removed v2's rows.
- A second copy of v2 is in `wiki-export-2024/Postgres_Failover.md`, an old
  wiki export that is also a source. Even with v2 gone, that copy would
  answer the same way.
- The answer showed no version or date, so I could not tell it was old.
